FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy an MES to monitor my machines?

No. We feed machine data into the systems you already use: ERP, MES, a database or an Excel sheet.

How much does a CNC control backup cost?

We quote control backups upfront, based on the control type (Fanuc, Heidenhain, Siemens Sinumerik). Backups of drives and other cabinet components are quoted separately after an inspection, because their number varies from machine to machine.

Why back up a CNC control?

Losing control data, for example after a battery runs flat or a memory card or drive fails, can stop a machine for a long time. A complete backup lets you restore parameters, PLC and programs and get back to production faster.

Where is the machine data stored?

In your company: on your servers and your network, following your IT department’s rules.
